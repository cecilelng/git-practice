# How to make a good cup of tea

This article will guide you through the steps to make a good cup of tea.


## Types of tea

There are different types of tea, each tea type has a unique flavor profile. The following table lists the different types of tea and their flavors.

| Tea type | Processing | Flavor |
| -------- | ---------- | ------ |
| [White tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tea) | Minimally processed; young buds and leaves are simply withered and dried in the sun. | Delicate, sweet, and floral. |
| [Green Tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_tea) | Unoxidized; the leaves are heated quickly (steamed in Japan or pan-fired in China) to stop oxidation. | Fresh, grassy, vegetal, or nutty. |
|[Yellow Tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_tea)|Rare Chinese tea made like green tea but given an extra damp-cloth wrapping (étouffage or men huan) step to gently steam and yellow the leaves.| Mellow, smoother, and less grassy than green tea |
|[Oolong Tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oolong)|Partially oxidized (roughly 10% to 85%) through a complex cycle of withering, bruising, and firing.| Ranges from bright and floral to creamy, woody, or roasted.|
|[Black Tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tea)| Fully oxidized (90% to 95%) and rolled to darken the leaves.|Robust, malty, full-bodied, or sweet-caramelly.|
|[Dark Tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermented_tea)|Post-fermented; microbial fermentation occurs naturally over years (raw/sheng) or is accelerated via pile-fermentation (ripe/shou).|Deeply earthy, woody, and smooth, often pressed into cakes|

## Prerequisites

To make a good cup of tea, make sure you have the following items:

- [ ] Good quality tea: loose leaf tea or a tea bag
- [ ] A tea mug
- [ ] A `Smeg` kettle or a pan
- [ ] A thermometer
- [ ] A tea filter if you are using tea leaves
- [ ] *(optional)* Some milk and sugar

## Making a good cup of tea

1. Based on your preference, pick one of the following tea form:
    - Loose leaf tea
    - Tea bag
2. From the tea instructions, find the recommended temperature and the infusion time.
3. Heat the water to the recommended temperature.
    > **Note:** Use a thermometer to reach the recommended temperature precisely.
4. Once the water reaches the recommended temperature, pour the water in the tea mug.
5. Depending on the selected tea form, do as follow:
   - **Loose leaf tea**: Add a teaspoon of loose leaf tea in the tea filter. Then put the tea filter in the tea mug.
   - **Tea bag**: Add the tea bag in the tea mug.
6. Let the tea infuse for the recommended infusion time.
7. *(optional)* Add milk and sugar based on your taste preferences.

You can now sit back, relax and enjoy a good cup of tea.
